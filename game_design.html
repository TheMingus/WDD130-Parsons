Game Design

Throughout my childhood I made simple games on a site called scratch before moving to a game engine called Godot in high school. My friends and I made a variety of projects, like Dodge A Brick or The Void

Think Outside the Box

Think Outside the Box is a non-euclidean Geometry puzzle platformer I made in 2021 while doing online school after covid. It’s confusing, simple, and non intuitive. It's very fun to watch people figure it out.

The void was my first “real” game I made, a simple platformer where a cat navigates a dark landscape by throwing paint. Made during 2020

Borrowed time is a side scroller rpg where a man uses time travel to catch a fey who “borrowed his time” developed in godot 4
